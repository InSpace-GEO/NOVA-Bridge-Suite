# Review-source branding

`google-maps.svg` is the original unmodified Google Maps DarkGray attribution logo supplied in Google's official attribution download:

https://developers.google.com/static/maps/documentation/images/Google_Maps_Attribution_Assets.zip

Source and display guidance: https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/javascript/policies#google_maps_logo_and_text_attribution

The renderer uses it solely to identify actual Google Maps sources, preserving its proportions and accessible label. Provider names and logos remain their respective owners' trademarks. Other publisher/provider images are configured by the client using the optional source logo field; they are not redistributed in this plugin.
